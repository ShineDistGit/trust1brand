Oral Care Education

A Better Routine Starts With The Basics

How Often Should You Replace Your Toothbrush?

Most dental professionals recommend replacing your toothbrush approximately every three months or sooner if the bristles become frayed. Worn bristles become less effective at removing plaque and food particles from teeth.

Quick Tips

  • ✓ Replace your toothbrush every 3 months
  • ✓ Replace after illness
  • ✓ Store upright when possible
  • ✓ Allow bristles to air dry between uses

Did You Know?

Frayed toothbrush bristles can reduce cleaning effectiveness and make it harder to reach certain areas of the teeth.

Soft vs Medium vs Hard Toothbrush

Soft toothbrushes are generally preferred for gentle everyday cleaning and may be especially comfortable for sensitive gums. Medium toothbrushes provide a firmer brushing experience while remaining suitable for everyday use. Hard toothbrushes offer a stronger brushing sensation and are preferred by some consumers, though brushing should always be performed gently.

How Long Should You Brush Your Teeth?

Dental professionals commonly recommend brushing for approximately two minutes twice daily. Dividing the mouth into four sections and brushing each area for around thirty seconds can help achieve a more complete clean.

Should You Use Mouthwash Every Day?

Mouthwash can be a useful addition to your daily oral-care routine. When used according to directions, it can help leave the mouth feeling clean and refreshed and complement regular brushing.

Simple Steps For A Better Oral-Care Routine

Brush twice daily, replace your toothbrush regularly, clean between teeth, use mouthwash as directed and maintain routine dental visits. Consistency is one of the most important aspects of good oral-care habits.
